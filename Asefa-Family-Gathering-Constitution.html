This constitution governs the quarterly gathering of the grandchildren of Asefa. It is written so that every member knows what is expected, so that the gathering runs without waiting on anyone, and so that no member has cause to be angry at another. The English text is followed by Amharic and Afaan Oromo versions. Where they differ, the Gathering decides which text controls.

ይህ መተዳደሪያ ደንብ የአሰፋ የልጅ ልጆች በየሦስት ወሩ የሚያደርጉትን የቤተሰብ ስብሰባ የሚመራ ነው። የተጻፈውም እያንዳንዱ አባል ከእሱ የሚጠበቀውን እንዲያውቅ፣ ስብሰባው ማንንም ሳይጠብቅ እንዲካሄድ፣ እንዲሁም አንዱ በሌላው ላይ የሚቆጣበት ምክንያት እንዳይኖር ነው። የእንግሊዝኛውን ጽሑፍ የአማርኛና የአፋን ኦሮሞ ቅጂዎች ይከተሉታል። ሲለያዩ የትኛው እንደሚጸና ስብሰባው ይወስናል።

Seerri ittiin bulmaataa kun walgahii ilmaan ijoollee Asefaa ji'a sadii sadiin taasifamu bulcha. Barreeffamni kun Afaan Ingilizii, Afaan Amaaraa fi Afaan Oromootiin dhihaateera. Isaan yoo wal dhaban, isa kamtu akka hojjetu Walgahiin murteessa.

Article 1Name and purpose

  1. The association is called the Asefa Family Gathering (the "Gathering").

  2. Its members are the grandchildren of Asefa, the children of his sons and daughters.

  3. The purpose of the Gathering is:

    • to keep the grandchildren of Asefa united as one family across generations;
    • to let the cousins and their children know one another and grow up together;
    • to support one another in times of joy and times of hardship;
    • to honour the memory and values of our grandfather Asefa and our parents.

  4. Every rule in this constitution serves the purpose above. When a rule is unclear, it is read in the way that best keeps the family in love and fairness.

  2. አባላቱ የአሰፋ የልጅ ልጆች፣ ማለትም የወንዶችና የሴቶች ልጆቹ ልጆች ናቸው።

Keewwata 1Maqaa fi kaayyoo

  1. Waldaan kun "Walgahii Maatii Asefaa" (kana booda "Walgahii") jedhamee waamama.

  2. Miseensonni isaa ilmaan ijoollee Asefaa, jechuun ijoollee ilmaan isaa fi intallan isaa ti.

  3. Kaayyoon Walgahii:

    • ilmaan ijoollee Asefaa dhaloota irraa gara dhalootaatti akka maatii tokkootti tokkummaadhaan eeguu;
    • ijoolleen obboleeyyanii fi ijoolleen isaanii wal beekanii waliin akka guddatan gochuu;
    • yeroo gammachuu fi yeroo rakkinaa wal gargaaruu;
    • yaadannoo fi duudhaa akaakayyuu keenya Asefaa fi warra keenyaa kabajuu.

  4. Seerri seera ittiin bulmaataa kana keessa jiru hundi kaayyoo armaan olii tajaajila. Seerri tokko ifa yoo hin taane, karaa maatii jaalalaa fi haqaan irra caalaa eeguun hiikama.

Article 2Membership

  1. Every grandchild of Asefa who has reached the age of 18 is a member and is expected to join.

  2. A member's husband or wife is a family member of the Gathering. They are welcome at every program, may speak in discussions, and may host together with the member. They do not pay a separate contribution and do not vote, unless the Gathering decides otherwise by two-thirds.

  3. Children of members attend with their parents. When a child of a member reaches 18, they may join as a full member with the same rights and duties.

  4. A member living outside the city or abroad remains a member. Their attendance and contribution arrangement is set under Article 7.

  5. A member may leave by writing to the Secretary. A member who leaves receives no refund of past contributions, since the fund belongs to the Gathering as a whole.

  6. A member who has not paid contributions or fines for six months, after two written reminders, is suspended. A suspended member may return by paying what is owed.

  7. Every member has the same rights: to attend, to speak, to vote, to host, to stand for the committee, and to see the records.

Keewwata 2Miseensummaa

  1. Ilmaan ijoollee Asefaa umuriin isaanii waggaa 18 guute hundi miseensa; akka makamanis ni eegama.

  2. Abbaan manaa yookiin haati manaa miseensaa miseensa maatii Walgahii ti. Sagantaa hunda irratti simatamu, marii irratti dubbachuu danda'u, miseensa waliin keessummeessuu danda'u. Buusii addaa hin kaffalan; Walgahiin sadii keessaa lamaan yoo murteesse malee sagalee hin kennan.

  3. Ijoolleen miseensotaa warra isaanii waliin argamu. Mucaan miseensaa waggaa 18 yoo guute, mirgaa fi dirqama walqixa qabaatee miseensa guutuu ta'ee makamuu danda'a.

  4. Miseensi magaalaa alaa yookiin biyya alaa jiraatu miseensummaan isaa itti fufa. Haalli argamuu fi buusii isaa Keewwata 7 jalatti murtaa'a.

  5. Miseensi barreessaadhaaf barreeffamaan beeksisee bahuu danda'a. Miseensi bahe buusii darbe hin deebifamuuf; sababiin isaas maallaqni kun qabeenya Walgahii guutuu waan ta'eefi.

  6. Miseensi ji'a jaha buusii yookiin adabbii hin kaffalle, akeekkachiisa barreeffamaa lama booda, ni uggurama. Miseensi uggurame waan irra jiru kaffaluun deebi'uu danda'a.

  7. Miseensi hundi mirga walqixa qaba: argamuu, dubbachuu, sagalee kennuu, keessummeessuu, koreef dorgomuu fi galmee ilaaluu.

Article 3The gathering

  1. The Gathering meets once every three months, on the first Saturday of that month.

  2. The program begins at 7:00 and ends at 11:00. The program starts at 7:00 whether or not every member has arrived. Nobody waits for anybody.

  3. The committee may move a date only for a death, a major holiday, or a national event, and must announce the new date at least two weeks ahead.

  4. Every program follows the same order so that it runs smoothly:

    7:00Opening and blessingHost
    7:10Welcome and roll callSecretary
    7:20Minutes of the last program and matters arisingSecretary
    7:30Treasurer's report: contributions, fines, balanceTreasurer
    7:45Family news: births, weddings, graduations, illness, needsLeader

  5. Any member who wants an item discussed sends it to the Secretary at least one week before the program. Items raised on the day are heard only if time allows.

  6. The formal part, from 7:00 to 8:40, is kept short and orderly so that the larger part of the day is for being together.

Article 4Hosting

  1. Each program is hosted by a married member in their own home, in the order fixed by lottery.

  2. The lottery is drawn once, in front of all members, and the Secretary records the full order. The order is not changed except as this article allows.

  3. Two hosts may swap turns by mutual agreement, with notice to the Secretary at least one month before the program.

  4. A host who cannot host on their turn for a serious reason tells the committee at least one month ahead. The next host in order steps forward, and the member who missed their turn hosts next.

  5. When every married member has hosted once, the cycle starts again in the same order. Members who married after the lottery are added at the end of the order.

  6. Unmarried members may volunteer to host or to co-host with a married member. A volunteer is added to the order at the next free turn.

  7. The host provides the place, the meal and the coffee ceremony, and keeps the program on time together with the Leader.

  8. Hosting is paid for by the host, as is our custom, but hosting is not a competition. The Gathering sets a reasonable spending guide so that no host feels pressured to outdo the last one. The Gathering may decide instead to pay a fixed hosting allowance from the fund.

  9. Every member thanks the host at the close of the program, and no member criticises the host's home or food.

Article 5The committee

  1. The Gathering is led by a committee of four: the Leader, the Deputy Leader, the Secretary and the Treasurer.

  2. Their duties are:

    • Leader: chairs the program, speaks for the Gathering, keeps order, and makes sure this constitution is followed.
    • Deputy Leader: acts for the Leader when absent, and coordinates with the host before each program.
    • Secretary: keeps the minutes, the attendance list, the hosting order and the fines register; sends notices and reminders.
    • Treasurer: collects contributions and fines, keeps the ledger and the bank account, and reports at every program.

  3. The committee is elected by simple majority of members present at the first program of the year. Each serves a two-year term and may be re-elected once in a row. After that they rest for at least one term.

  4. Any member in good standing, with no unpaid dues, may stand for any post.

  5. The Gathering may remove a committee member by two-thirds vote for dishonesty, repeated absence, or neglect of duty, after hearing them.

  6. If a post falls vacant, the committee appoints a member to fill it until the next election.

  7. Committee members are not paid and are not exempt from contributions or fines.

Article 6Finances

  1. Every member contributes 700 birr each month. Payment is due by the 10th of the month, into the Gathering's bank account or to the Treasurer against a receipt.

  2. The Gathering keeps one bank account in its own name. Withdrawals need two signatures: the Treasurer and the Leader (or the Deputy Leader).

  3. The fund is used only for:

    • support to a member at a wedding, a birth, a serious illness, or a death in their close family, in the amounts fixed in the support schedule;
    • an emergency loan to a member in hardship, approved by the Gathering and repaid on agreed terms;
    • joint family events and the running costs of the Gathering itself (stationery, receipts, bank charges);
    • any other purpose approved by two-thirds of members present.

  4. The support schedule is set by the Gathering and reviewed every year:

    EventSupport from the fund (birr)
    Wedding of a member
    Birth of a member's child
    Death of a member's parent, spouse or child
    Serious illness or hospital stay of a member

  5. No payment leaves the fund without a written request, the Treasurer's record and the Leader's approval. Payments above the schedule need a vote of the Gathering.

  6. The Treasurer reports at every program: contributions received, fines received, payments made, and the closing balance. The report is written and attached to the minutes.

  7. Two members who are not on the committee audit the ledger and the bank statements once a year and report to the Gathering.

  8. A contribution more than 30 days overdue carries a late fee of 100 birr per month.

Article 7Attendance and fines

  1. Attendance at every program is a duty of membership.

  2. A member who cannot attend tells the Secretary at least three days before the program.

  3. Fines are as follows:

    SituationFine (birr)
    Absent without notice2,000
    Absent with notice given at least 3 days before1,000
    Absent for an excused reason (see clause 4)none
    Late by more than 30 minutes without notice200
    Disturbing the program after one warning from the Leader500
    Host cancelling their turn with less than one month's notice, without a serious reason1,000

  4. Excused reasons are: the member's own illness or the illness of their spouse or child; a death or funeral; a birth; work travel; an exam; and a national or religious duty. The Secretary records the reason. The committee may accept other serious reasons.

  5. Members living outside the city or abroad are excused from attendance and may join by video call. They continue to pay contributions unless the Gathering agrees a different amount.

  6. A fine is recorded by the Secretary on the day and announced at the next program. It is paid before or at the next program.

  7. A member who disputes a fine raises it with the committee within two weeks. The committee decides, and the member may appeal to the full Gathering under Article 10.

  8. A member with an unpaid fine does not vote until it is paid. Unpaid fines are treated like unpaid contributions under Article 2.

  9. Fines go into the fund and are reported together with the contributions.

Article 8Code of conduct

  1. We speak to one another with respect, as brothers and sisters. No insults, no shouting, no mocking.

  2. We respect the host's home as our own: we arrive on time, we help where asked, and we leave the place as we found it.

  3. Phones are on silent during the formal part of the program.

  4. Political and business arguments are not held during the program.

  5. Parents are responsible for their children's behaviour and safety.

  6. Alcohol, if served, is taken in moderation. A member who becomes disorderly is asked to leave and is fined under Article 7.

  7. What a member shares in confidence at the Gathering stays within the family.

  8. A member who has a complaint about another raises it under Article 10, not in front of the Gathering and not on social media.

  9. The Leader may give one warning for a breach. A second breach in the same program is fined. A pattern of breaches is brought to the Gathering, which may suspend the member for one program.

Article 9Decisions and voting

  1. The Gathering decides as a whole. The committee carries out decisions; it does not make them alone, except for day-to-day matters.

  2. Quorum: decisions are valid only when more than half of the members are present.

  3. Ordinary matters pass by simple majority of members present. A tie is decided by the Leader.

  4. These matters need two-thirds of members present: changing the contribution amount, changing a fine, spending above the support schedule, suspending or removing a member or a committee member, and amending this constitution.

  5. Voting is by show of hands. Any three members may ask for a secret ballot.

  6. Each member has one vote. There is no voting by proxy, except that a member abroad may vote in writing on a matter announced in advance.

  7. A matter once decided is not reopened for one year unless new facts arise.

Article 10Settling disputes

  1. Disagreements are expected in any family. What matters is how they are settled.

  2. The steps, in order:

    • Step 1. The two members talk privately and try to settle it themselves within two weeks.
    • Step 2. If not settled, either member asks the committee in writing. The committee hears both within one month and proposes a settlement.
    • Step 3. If either member rejects the committee's proposal, the matter goes to the full Gathering at the next program. The Gathering hears both and decides by simple majority. Both members leave the room during the vote.
    • Step 4. If the dispute involves a committee member, or the Gathering's decision is not accepted, the matter goes to an elder of the parents' generation chosen by the Gathering. The elder's word is final.

  3. No dispute is discussed on social media or with people outside the family.

  4. A member who refuses to take part in these steps, or who takes a family dispute to outsiders, is fined 1,000 birr and may be suspended.

  5. When a dispute is settled, the two members shake hands before the Gathering, and the matter is closed and recorded.

Article 11Records and transparency

  1. The Secretary keeps the member register, the attendance list, the minutes of every program, the hosting order, and the fines register.

  2. The Treasurer keeps the contribution ledger, receipts, the bank statements, and the payments register.

  3. Minutes are read at the next program and approved by the members.

  4. Any member may ask to see any record at any time, and the committee shows it within one week.

  5. Records are kept in a shared folder, on paper and digitally, that passes to the next committee at every election.

  6. A copy of this constitution is given to every member, and to every new member on joining.

Article 12Amendments and adoption

  1. Any member may propose an amendment in writing to the Secretary. The proposal is announced at one program and voted on at the next, so that every member has three months to consider it.

  2. An amendment passes by two-thirds of members present and takes effect from the following program.

  3. This constitution is adopted when it is read in full at a program, approved by two-thirds of all members, and signed by the committee and the members present.

  4. Matters the Gathering must decide at adoption:

    • the hosting spending guide or hosting allowance (Article 4, clause 8);
    • the amounts in the support schedule (Article 6, clause 4);
    • whether spouses vote (Article 2, clause 2);
    • the contribution for members living abroad (Article 7, clause 5);
    • the elder for final appeal (Article 10).

  1. ማንኛውም አባል ማሻሻያ በጽሑፍ ለጸሐፊው ማቅረብ ይችላል። ሐሳቡ በአንድ ፕሮግራም ይገለጻል፤ በሚቀጥለው ድምፅ ይሰጥበታል፤ ይህም እያንዳንዱ አባል ለማሰብ ሦስት ወር እንዲኖረው ነው።

  2. ማሻሻያ በተገኙ አባላት ሁለት ሦስተኛ ድምፅ ያልፋል፤ ከሚቀጥለው ፕሮግራም ጀምሮ ተግባራዊ ይሆናል።

  3. ይህ ደንብ የሚጸድቀው በአንድ ፕሮግራም ሙሉ በሙሉ ተነቦ፣ ከሁሉም አባላት ሁለት ሦስተኛ ሲያጸድቁት እና በኮሚቴውና በተገኙ አባላት ሲፈረም ነው።

  4. ስብሰባው ሲያጸድቅ መወሰን ያለበት ጉዳዮች፦

    • የማስተናገድ የወጪ መመሪያ ወይም ድጎማ (አንቀጽ 4፣ ቁጥር 8)፤
    • የድጋፍ ሠንጠረዥ መጠኖች (አንቀጽ 6፣ ቁጥር 4)፤
    • ባልና ሚስት ድምፅ ይስጡ አይስጡ (አንቀጽ 2፣ ቁጥር 2)፤
    • በውጭ አገር ላሉ አባላት መዋጮ (አንቀጽ 7፣ ቁጥር 5)፤
    • ለመጨረሻ ይግባኝ የሚሆን ሽማግሌ (አንቀጽ 10)።

Keewwata 12Fooyya'iinsa fi raggaasisuu

  1. Miseensi kamiyyuu fooyya'iinsa barreeffamaan barreessaaf dhiheessuu danda'a. Yaadni sagantaa tokko irratti beeksifamee isa itti aanu irratti sagaleen kennama; kunis miseensi hundi itti yaaduuf ji'a sadii akka qabaatuuf.

  2. Fooyya'iinsi sagalee sadii keessaa lama miseensota argamaniitiin darba; sagantaa itti aanu irraa eegalee hojii irra oola.

  3. Seerri ittiin bulmaataa kun kan raggaasifamu sagantaa tokko irratti guutummaatti dubbifamee, miseensota hundaa keessaa sadii keessaa lamaan raggaasifamee, koree fi miseensota argamaniin yoo mallatteeffame dha.

  4. Dhimmoota Walgahiin yeroo raggaasisu murteessuu qabu:

    • qajeelfama baasii keessummeessuu yookiin deeggarsa keessummeessuu (Keewwata 4, lakk. 8);
    • hanga gabatee deeggarsaa (Keewwata 6, lakk. 4);
    • abbaan manaa fi haati manaa sagalee kennuu fi dhiisuu (Keewwata 2, lakk. 2);
    • buusii miseensota biyya alaa jiraataniif (Keewwata 7, lakk. 5);
    • jaarsa ol-iyyannoo dhumaaf (Keewwata 10).
