Family Gamesየቤተሰብ ጨዋታዎችTaphoota maatii

Two games for the whole family. Each one is played on a single phone.ለመላው ቤተሰብ ሁለት ጨዋታዎች። እያንዳንዱ በአንድ ስልክ ይጫወታል።Taphoota lama maatii guutuuf. Tokkoon tokkoon isaanii bilbila tokkoon taphatama.

Enkulal!ዕንቁላል!Hanqaaquu!

Pass the egg around the circle as fast as you can. It bursts at a random moment, and whoever holds it does a forfeit.ዕንቁላሉን በክቡ ውስጥ በተቻለ ፍጥነት አሳልፉ። በማይታወቅ ጊዜ ይፈነዳል፤ የያዘው ሰው ቅጣት ይፈጽማል።Hanqaaquu geengoo keessatti hamma dandeessan saffisaan dabarsaa. Yeroo hin beekamnetti dhoha; namni qabate adabbii raawwata.

Tekula!ተኩላ!Yeeyyii!

Wolves hide among the family, the doctor saves, and everyone votes. A narrator runs the nights from one phone.ተኩላዎች በቤተሰቡ መካከል ተደብቀዋል፤ ሐኪሙ ያድናል፣ ሁሉም ድምፅ ይሰጣል። ተራኪው ሌሊቶቹን በአንድ ስልክ ይመራል።Yeeyyiiwwan maatii keessa dhokataniiru; ogeessi fayyaa ni baraara, hundi sagalee kenna. Seenessaan halkan bilbila tokkoon geggeessa.

Before you playከመጫወትዎ በፊትOsoo hin taphatin dura

  • Turn the phone's sound up.የስልኩን ድምፅ ከፍ ያድርጉ።Sagalee bilbilaa ol kaasi.
  • Set the screen to stay on for a few minutes.ማያው ለጥቂት ደቂቃዎች እንዳይጠፋ ያስተካክሉ።Iskiriiniin daqiiqaa muraasaaf akka hin dhamne qindeessi.
  • Names you add are remembered on this phone.ያስገቧቸው ስሞች በዚህ ስልክ ላይ ይቀመጣሉ።Maqaan galchite bilbila kana irratti ni yaadatama.
